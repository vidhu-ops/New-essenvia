## Executive summary (original)

Essentra turns recurring household shopping into **one simple monthly price**. Customers keep brands they trust. You get recurring revenue and retention data by rep and channel.

**One platform:** website, customer app, rep tablet, admin, warehouse—one database.

**Package engine:** wholesale costs are internal; customers see one price. Admin controls names, prices, thresholds.

**Door-to-door:** rep flow → tap payment → account, subscription, order, attribution.

**Growth engine:** brand, research, social, SEO, paid, lifecycle automation (quoted separately).

**Low risk start:** Phase 0 design + fixed quote before major build spend.
