console, together with supporting back-end systems (the “Platform”).

develop, test, deploy, document and support the Platform.

sections are the subject of this Agreement (the “Proposal”). By email dated [●] October 2026

provide the Services, in this Agreement, having regard to the laws of India and Canada that
affect a cross-border engagement of this nature.

3.SCOPE OF SERVICES
3.1The Service Provider shall design, develop, test, deploy and document the Platform in eight (8)
Phases, being Phase 0 to Phase 7, as summarised in Schedule A, and shall perform the other
Services described in this Agreement.
3.2The only agreed price for the full Platform (Phases 0 to 7) is the Development Fee of CAD
2,975.00.
3.3Full scope retained. The Development Fee is agreed on the footing that the full platform scope
described in the Proposal remains included. Any functionality that is described in the
Proposal, and any functionality that is not separately described but is reasonably necessary for
a described feature to operate as intended, is within scope and shall not give rise to any
additional charge. A requirement that goes beyond those limits shall be treated as a Change
Request under clause 19. Where the Proposal and Schedule A differ as to functional scope,
the Proposal prevails. Lifecycle email and SMS flows and the analytics and attribution set-up,
which the Proposal states are included in Phases 2, 3 and 5, form part of the Services.
3.4Standard of performance. The Service Provider shall perform the Services (a) with reasonable
skill, care and diligence, in accordance with good industry practice for the development of
commercial software platforms, (b) using appropriately qualified and experienced personnel,
and (c) in compliance with Applicable Law.
3.5No dilution of quality. The Parties agree that the twelve (12) week timeline shall not be achieved at
the expense of testing, security or core functionality. The Service Provider shall not omit or
materially reduce any testing activity or security control described in Schedule A in order to
meet any milestone. If the Service Provider considers that a milestone cannot be met without
such a reduction, it shall notify the Client promptly and the Parties shall agree a revised
milestone under clause 5.
3.6Included items. The Development Fee includes, without additional charge, the set-up of the
development, staging and production environments (the provider charges for which are
Third-Party Costs under clause 6.8), deployment to production, the documentation and
Handover Pack, the walkthrough sessions described in Schedule A, the internal testing
described in clause 9.1 and Part 4 of Schedule A, correction of Defects in accordance with
this Agreement, and Hypercare Services during the Hypercare Period.

Provider shall notify the Client in writing within two (2) Indian Business Days of becoming
